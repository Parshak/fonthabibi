How it works

  1. 1

    Say what you're building

    Restaurant, salon, gym, law firm, portfolio. There are 24 kinds of business to pick from.

  2. 2

    Choose a kit

    Each kit is drawn as a small website, so you judge the fonts and colors the way your visitors will see them.

  3. 3

    Copy it

    Copy for AI hands the whole kit to any AI tool. Copy CSS gives you the variables for your own stylesheet.

What Copy for AI gives you

A short brief any AI tool can follow: the fonts to load, which color goes where, and the CSS variables. Paste it at the top of your prompt and everything it builds uses your kit.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no paywall and no limits.

Can I use a kit in client or commercial work?

Yes. Every font is a Google font, free for personal and commercial use, and the color sets are yours to use anywhere. No credit needed.

Which AI tools does it work with?

All of them. Copy for AI is plain text, so it works in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor, v0, Lovable, Bolt, or anywhere else you can type a prompt. Paste it at the top and the AI knows which fonts and colors to use and what each one is for.

Can I change a kit?

Yes. Press Customize on any kit to open it in the mixer, swap the fonts or the colors, and copy the result.

What do AA and AAA contrast mean?

They are the accessibility levels for readable text. AA means every text color has a contrast of at least 4.5 to 1 against its background. AAA means the main text reaches 7 to 1.
