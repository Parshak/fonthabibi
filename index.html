FAQ

Quick answers.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

Really free, no catch, no account. It's a personal project I built for my own work and decided to share.

How do I use a kit with Claude or Cursor?

Copy the kit's CSS or JSON from the mixer and paste it into your prompt with: "Use these design tokens for everything you build." The AI inherits your fonts, colors, and radii instead of inventing generic ones.

Are the fonts free to use?

Yes. Every voice uses Google Fonts, which are free for personal and commercial projects. Each export includes the exact import line.

Can I use kits in client or commercial work?

Yes, unlimited projects, personal or commercial, no attribution needed. Go make things.
